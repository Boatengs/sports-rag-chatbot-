LATEST TENNIS NEWS 2024-2025
Carlos Alcaraz won Wimbledon 2024 defeating Novak Djokovic in the final.
Jannik Sinner won the Australian Open 2024 and US Open 2024.
Jannik Sinner became world number one in 2024.
Novak Djokovic won the Olympic gold medal in Paris 2024.
Rafael Nadal retired from professional tennis in November 2024.
Iga Swiatek remains dominant in women's tennis as world number one.
Coco Gauff won the US Open 2023 and is a top contender in 2024-25.
