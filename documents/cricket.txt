CRICKET - HISTORY AND LATEST NEWS

FORMATS OF CRICKET
Test Cricket - 5 day format, considered the pinnacle of cricket
One Day International (ODI) - 50 overs per side
T20 International - 20 overs per side, most exciting format

GREATEST CRICKET PLAYERS OF ALL TIME
Sachin Tendulkar (India) - Most runs in international cricket, 100 international centuries
Don Bradman (Australia) - Greatest batsman ever, Test average of 99.94
Muttiah Muralitharan (Sri Lanka) - Most wickets in Test cricket (800 wickets)
Shane Warne (Australia) - Greatest leg spin bowler, 708 Test wickets
Vivian Richards (West Indies) - Greatest batsman of his era
Brian Lara (West Indies) - Holds record for highest individual Test score of 400 not out

MOST SUCCESSFUL TEST NATIONS
Australia - Most World Cup titles in ODI cricket (6 titles)
West Indies - Dominated cricket in 1970s and 1980s
India - Most powerful cricket nation currently by revenue and fanbase
England - Birthplace of cricket

ICC CRICKET WORLD CUP 2023
India hosted the 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup.
Australia won the 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup defeating India in the final.
Travis Head was the player of the match in the final with a brilliant century.
India were unbeaten throughout the tournament before losing the final.
Virat Kohli was the top scorer of the tournament.

ICC T20 WORLD CUP 2024
India won the ICC T20 World Cup 2024 held in the West Indies and USA.
India defeated South Africa in the final by 7 runs in a thrilling match.
Virat Kohli scored a crucial 76 in the final.
Rohit Sharma led India to the title.
It ended India's 11 year wait for an ICC tournament title.

IPL - INDIAN PREMIER LEAGUE
The IPL is the most valuable cricket league in the world.
Kolkata Knight Riders won the IPL 2024 title.
The IPL features the best players from around the world.
Virat Kohli plays for Royal Challengers Bangalore.
MS Dhoni continues to play for Chennai Super Kings.
