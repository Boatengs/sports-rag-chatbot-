THE SUPER BOWL
The Super Bowl is the annual championship game of the NFL.
It is the most watched sporting event in the United States every year.

Super Bowl LVIII (2024) - Kansas City Chiefs defeated San Francisco 49ers 25-22 in overtime
Super Bowl LVII (2023) - Kansas City Chiefs defeated Philadelphia Eagles 38-35
Super Bowl LVI (2022) - Los Angeles Rams defeated Cincinnati Bengals 23-20
Super Bowl LV (2021) - Tampa Bay Buccaneers defeated Kansas City Chiefs 31-9

PATRICK MAHOMES ERA
Patrick Mahomes is the current best quarterback in the NFL.
Mahomes has won 3 Super Bowls with the Kansas City Chiefs (2020, 2023, 2024).
Mahomes won back to back Super Bowls in 2023 and 2024.
Travis Kelce is Mahomes top target and tight end for the Chiefs.
Travis Kelce is in a relationship with Taylor Swift since 2023.

NFL 2024-2025 SEASON
The Kansas City Chiefs are seeking an unprecedented third consecutive Super Bowl.
Super Bowl LIX is scheduled for February 2025 in New Orleans.
Lamar Jackson of the Baltimore Ravens won the 2024 NFL MVP award.
The Detroit Lions are one of the surprise contenders in 2024-25.
