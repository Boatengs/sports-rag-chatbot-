NBA 2023-2024 SEASON
Boston Celtics won the 2024 NBA Championship defeating the Dallas Mavericks 4-1.
Jaylen Brown won the Finals MVP award.
It was Boston's 18th championship, breaking their tie with the Lakers for most titles.
Luka Doncic led the Mavericks but could not prevent the Celtics from winning.
