GLOBAL BASKETBALL AND OTHER TOP SPORTS LEAGUES

FIBA BASKETBALL WORLD CUP 2023
Germany won the 2023 FIBA Basketball World Cup held in the Philippines, Japan and Indonesia.
Germany defeated Serbia 83-77 in the final.
Dennis Schroder was the star player for Germany.
USA finished fourth, their worst ever result in a major basketball tournament.

PARIS OLYMPICS 2024 BASKETBALL
USA won the gold medal in basketball at the 2024 Paris Olympics.
USA defeated France 98-87 in the final on French home soil.
LeBron James won his first Olympic gold as team captain.
Stephen Curry was the star of the final with 36 points.

FORMULA 1 2024
Max Verstappen won his fourth consecutive Formula 1 World Championship in 2024.
Verstappen drives for Red Bull Racing.
Lando Norris of McLaren was his closest rival in 2024.
Lewis Hamilton announced he is moving to Ferrari in 2025.
Lewis Hamilton has 7 Formula 1 World Championships, tied with Michael Schumacher for most ever.

MLB BASEBALL 2024
Los Angeles Dodgers won the 2024 World Series.
Shohei Ohtani signed with the LA Dodgers in 2023 for a record 700 million dollar contract.
Ohtani won the NL MVP in 2024 and World Series with the Dodgers.
Freddie Freeman hit a walk-off grand slam in Game 1 of the 2024 World Series.

NHL ICE HOCKEY 2024
Florida Panthers won the 2024 Stanley Cup Championship.
Panthers defeated the Edmonton Oilers 4-3 in the series after being down 3-0.
It was one of the greatest comebacks in Stanley Cup Finals history.
Connor McDavid of Edmonton Oilers is considered the best player in the NHL.

UFC AND MMA 2024
Jon Jones remains the UFC Heavyweight Champion.
Islam Makhachev is the UFC Lightweight Champion.
Alex Pereira has become a star winning multiple weight class titles.
The UFC continues to grow globally as one of the most popular sports organizations.

RUGBY WORLD CUP 2023
South Africa won the Rugby World Cup 2023 held in France.
South Africa defeated New Zealand 12-11 in the final.
It was South Africa's fourth Rugby World Cup title.
New Zealand are the most successful Rugby World Cup team with 3 titles.
