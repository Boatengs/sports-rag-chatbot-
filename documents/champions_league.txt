UEFA CHAMPIONS LEAGUE HISTORY AND STATISTICS

The UEFA Champions League is the most prestigious club football competition in the world.

ALL TIME WINNERS
Real Madrid - 15 titles (most successful club)
AC Milan - 7 titles
Bayern Munich - 6 titles
Liverpool - 6 titles
Barcelona - 5 titles
Manchester United - 3 titles
Chelsea - 2 titles
Juventus - 2 titles

RECENT FINALS
2023 - Manchester City defeated Inter Milan 1-0
2022 - Real Madrid defeated Liverpool 1-0
2021 - Chelsea defeated Manchester City 1-0
2020 - Bayern Munich defeated PSG 1-0
2019 - Liverpool defeated Tottenham 2-0

ALL TIME TOP SCORERS
Cristiano Ronaldo - 140 goals (all time record)
Lionel Messi - 129 goals
Robert Lewandowski - 100 goals
Karim Benzema - 90 goals
Raul - 71 goals

GREATEST COMEBACKS
2005 Final - Liverpool vs AC Milan: Liverpool came back from 3-0 down at half time to win on penalties
2019 Semi Final - Liverpool vs Barcelona: Liverpool won 4-0 after losing 3-0 at Camp Nou
2017 Round of 16 - Barcelona vs PSG: Barcelona won 6-1 after losing 4-0 in Paris
