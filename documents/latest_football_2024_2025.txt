LATEST FOOTBALL NEWS AND STATS 2024-2025

BALLON D'OR 2024
Rodri of Manchester City won the 2024 Ballon d'Or award.
It was the first time since 2018 that neither Messi nor Ronaldo won the award.
Vinicius Junior of Real Madrid finished second.
Lionel Messi finished fifth despite winning the Copa America 2024 with Argentina.
Rodri won the Ballon d'Or because of his outstanding performances for Manchester City and Spain.
Spain won Euro 2024 with Rodri as a key player.

KYLIAN MBAPPE JOINS REAL MADRID 2024
Kylian Mbappe joined Real Madrid on a free transfer from PSG in summer 2024.
Mbappe signed a 5 year deal with Real Madrid.
This ended years of speculation about his future.
Mbappe is considered one of the best players in the world alongside Vinicius Junior.
Real Madrid now have Mbappe, Vinicius Junior, and Bellingham as their attacking trio.

UEFA EURO 2024
Spain won UEFA Euro 2024 held in Germany.
Spain defeated England 2-1 in the final in Berlin.
Lamine Yamal scored a stunning equalizer for Spain in the final at just 16 years old.
Mikel Oyarzabal scored the winning goal for Spain in the 86th minute.
Rodri won the player of the tournament award at Euro 2024.

COPA AMERICA 2024
Argentina won the Copa America 2024 held in the United States.
Argentina defeated Colombia 1-0 in the final through a goal in extra time.
Lionel Messi played but was injured during the final.
Lautaro Martinez was the top scorer of the tournament.

UEFA CHAMPIONS LEAGUE 2024-2025
Real Madrid are the defending Champions League champions.
The 2024-25 Champions League introduced a new league phase format with 36 teams.
Kylian Mbappe is playing his first Champions League season with Real Madrid in 2024-25.

PREMIER LEAGUE 2024-2025
Manchester City are the defending Premier League champions.
Liverpool under new manager Arne Slot after Jurgen Klopp retired.
Jurgen Klopp left Liverpool after 9 years at the end of 2023-24 season.
Arsenal are strong title contenders in 2024-25.

FIFA CLUB WORLD CUP 2025
The expanded FIFA Club World Cup with 32 teams is scheduled for summer 2025 in the USA.
Real Madrid, Manchester City, Chelsea, and Inter Miami are participating clubs.
